# Seedance Prompts: EVAplate Vacuumverdamping Procesvideo

## Optie 1: Abstract/Artistiek Procesoverzicht (aanbevolen voor hero section)

```
A cinematic close-up visualization of an industrial vacuum evaporation process. 
The camera slowly pushes into a transparent vessel where contaminated liquid 
gently begins to bubble and transform into rising vapor at low temperature. 
Soft blue and green lighting emphasizes sustainability. The vapor rises 
gracefully upward through clean industrial tubing, then contacts a cool 
condensation surface where crystal-clear water droplets form and collect — 
pure and pristine. The scene transitions from dark murky wastewater on one 
side to brilliant clean water on the other. Shallow depth of field, 
cinematic color grading with teal and white tones, smooth slow-motion 
movement. Industrial yet elegant. No text overlays.
```

## Optie 2: Macro/Wetenschappelijk Perspectief

```
Extreme macro cinematic shot of the evaporation and condensation cycle. 
A dark industrial liquid inside a sealed plastic chamber begins to evaporate 
under vacuum pressure — tiny bubbles form and rise as molecules escape the 
surface at low temperature. The camera follows the invisible water vapor 
upward in slow motion, transitioning to a condensation zone where the vapor 
touches a cooled surface and transforms back into pure, crystal-clear water 
droplets that slowly roll down and collect. Moody blue-green lighting, 
volumetric fog effects, ultra-smooth camera movement. Scientific yet 
beautiful. Sustainable industrial aesthetic with clean modern feel.
```

## Optie 3: Full Industrieel Overzicht

```
Smooth cinematic flythrough of a modern industrial vacuum evaporation system 
made from engineering plastic. Contaminated wastewater flows into the compact 
machine. Inside, under vacuum conditions, the liquid evaporates at low 
temperature — visible as gentle rising steam. The clean vapor travels through 
plastic conduits to a condensation unit where it transforms into pure clear 
water, flowing out as recovered clean water. A concentrated residue remains, 
showing 95% volume reduction. The scene is lit with cool teal and warm 
amber accent lighting. Sleek industrial design, sustainable technology feel, 
slow deliberate camera movements. Professional corporate video aesthetic.
```

## Optie 4: Korte Loop (perfect voor achtergrondvideo)

```
Seamless looping close-up of water evaporation and condensation in a clean 
industrial setting. Liquid gently bubbles and releases vapor that rises 
slowly upward, then condenses as pristine water droplets on a smooth surface. 
Soft blue-green ambient lighting, shallow depth of field, slow motion. 
Calming and hypnotic. Sustainable technology aesthetic. Clean and minimal.
```

## Tips voor Seedance

- **Resolutie**: Kies de hoogste beschikbare resolutie (voor website hero section)
- **Duur**: 4-6 seconden is ideaal voor een website hero background video
- **Stijl**: Gebruik "cinematic" of "professional" style preset als beschikbaar
- **Negatieve prompt** (als Seedance dit ondersteunt): `text, watermark, logo, cartoon, anime, low quality, blurry, shaky camera, people, faces, hands`
- **Aspect ratio**: 16:9 voor hero section, 1:1 voor social media
- Genereer meerdere variaties en kies de beste
